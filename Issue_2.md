# Issue 2 — Make the theme yours (easy → medium)

Give this music app a more personal visual identity.

## Your task

1. Change `accent` and `background` in `app/src/theme.json`. Choose readable colours.
2. Edit one shape or colour in `app/public/assets/theme.svg`. SVG is just text describing shapes!
3. Change the short `description` to welcome your community.

Keep the ODE logo and event name. Do not use remote images: the app must look good offline.

## Done when

- Text and buttons remain easy to read, with visible keyboard focus.
- The illustration matches your theme and appears offline.
- The page works on a narrow screen.
